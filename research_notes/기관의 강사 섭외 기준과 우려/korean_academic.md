## Q1. 외부강사 선정 기준: 무엇이 있고 어떻게 순위/가중치가 매겨지는가

### Takeaway
Only one Korean study was found that directly ranks how corporate HRD staff select instructors: 권윤정·이영민 (2020), a conjoint analysis. Its order of importance is **강의경력 > 기업재직경력 > 강사료 > 성별**. The authors' framing point is that selection in practice is "비체계적이고 개인 네트워크에 의존". I found no AHP study that weights external-instructor selection criteria. Other evidence on criteria is indirect: learner-side evaluation criteria, and studies linking instructor characteristics to learner satisfaction.

### Cited Findings
- **권윤정·이영민 (2020), 「기업교육 강사 선정 요인에 관한 컨조인트 분석」, 『기업교육과인재연구』 22(3), pp.203–229.** The study ran a conjoint analysis on 기업교육 담당자 to identify the 선정 요인 they weigh when choosing instructors and each factor's 상대적 중요도. Its stated purpose is to secure "강사 선정 방식의 체계적ㆍ합리적 기준을 마련하는 데 필요한 기초 자료". — [KCI ART002629706](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002629706)
  - Result as excerpted: "교육담당자들이 가장 중요하다고 판단한 강사선택 속성은 **강의경력, 기업재직 경력, 강사료, 성별**의 순으로 나타났다." — [KCI ART002629706](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002629706). The exact %-importance values and part-worths could not be retrieved, because the KCI page is not reachable from this session. One search pass reported that a citing thesis's table was garbled, so treat the ranking as abstract-level.
  - Problem statement: "강사 선정의 과정과 방식이 비체계적이고 개인 네트워크에 의존하기 때문이다" — [KCI ART002629706](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002629706)
- **박지아 (2014), 「기업교육 강사 선정기준에 관한 기업교육 담당자의 인식 연구」** (학위논문). It appears in reference lists, but I could not find its abstract or results. — the title was surfaced via search results alongside the KCI conjoint paper ([KCI ART002629706](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002629706)). Needs a direct check in RISS.
- **한성대 박사학위논문, 「기업교육 프리랜서 강사의 사회적 네트워크와 고용가능성의 관계 – 온라인 네트워크의 조절효과와 지식공유의 매개효과를 중심으로」.** It treats 사회적 네트워크 (offline and online) as a driver of freelance corporate instructors' employability (being hired). This matches the conjoint paper's finding that hiring runs through personal networks. I could not retrieve the specific coefficients or year. — [Hansung dspace PDF](https://dspace.hansung.ac.kr/bitstream/2024.oak/6717/2/200000726727.pdf)
- **김영화·전도근·최종철 (2007), 「성인학습자 관점에서 접근한 평생교육강사 평가준거 연구」, 『한국교육』 34(2), pp.155–186 (KEDI).** It builds criteria for evaluating 평생교육 강사 from a job analysis. One abstract extraction said instructor qualities were grouped into **7 domains**, and EFA/CFA produced **12 evaluation criteria** ("twelve evaluation criteria were extracted"). A second search could not reconfirm the 7/12 counts, so verify them. — [KCI ART001085937](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART001085937)
- **「평생교육 강사의 평가 준거 및 척도 개발 연구」 (KRF/KRM 기초학문자료센터).** It surveyed 1,000 learners at 평생교육기관 and used 934 responses for EFA/CFA. The 자질 영역 include 성인학습자 특성 이해, 프로그램 개발, 교수기술, 학습(촉진) and others; the full list was truncated. It is likely the funded project behind the 2007 paper above, but that link is unconfirmed. — [KRM](https://www.krm.or.kr/krmts/search/detailview/research.html?dbGubun=SD&m201_id=10011946)
- A 50+ program evaluation-indicator report cites the 2007 study for factors such as 유익성, 편의성, 친밀성, 자기주도적 학습 지원성, 외형성. I could not confirm whether these are that paper's instructor criteria or program-level criteria. — [서울시50플러스재단 50+교육프로그램 평가지표 개발 (2019)](https://www.50plus.or.kr/upload/im/2019/04/48174e2e-250a-4db9-a81b-6be12aae34dc.pdf)
- **Instructor characteristics and learner satisfaction (indirect evidence on which criteria matter):**
  - 바리스타교육 강사 study (미래융합통섭학회): "강사의 전문성 보다는 강사의 교수기법, 강사프로그램 개발 및 수업계획 등이 체계적일 경우 교육의 만족은 높은 것으로 나타났다". Instructor qualities also affected 재수강 의도. — [KISS 3803782](https://kiss.kstudy.com/Detail/Ar?key=3803782)
  - 창업교육 서비스 품질 study: 전문적인 강사 was correlated with 교육만족도 (r ≈ .810 as reported in the extraction). — [KCI ART001924074](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART001924074)
  - 직업훈련 교강사 특성별 훈련생 만족도 차이 분석 (한국산학기술학회논문지 24(3)): satisfaction differs by instructor 성별, 연령, 실무경력. — [KAIS PDF](https://www.kais99.org/jkais/journal/Vol24No03/vol24no03p31.pdf)
  - 직업훈련 교·강사의 특성이 훈련성과에 미치는 영향: tests instructor characteristics against training outcomes such as 취업률, using regression. — [KCI ART002827979](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002827979)
  - 직업훈련교사 단기교육 만족도 결정요인 (개도국 초청연수): satisfaction with the instructor's 이론 지식 수준 was higher than satisfaction with 실습/OJT. — [KoreaScience JAKO201217443315520](https://koreascience.or.kr/article/JAKO201217443315520.pdf)
- **AHP:** No Korean AHP study weighting external-instructor selection criteria turned up. The AHP studies found cover adjacent topics: 상업계 특성화고 교육서비스 요소 ([KCI ART002379576](https://kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002379576)) and 평생교육사 직무 중요도 ([KCI ART001641897](https://kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART001641897)).

### Inferences
- In corporate HRD, the "hard" proxies (강의경력, 기업재직경력, and cost via 강사료) outrank the softer qualities the user listed (평판, 강의평가, 커리큘럼 맞춤성). This is consistent with buyers who lack a reliable way to observe teaching quality before hiring and so rely on credentials and networks.
- The satisfaction literature points the other way: 교수기법 and 수업계획/프로그램 설계 predict satisfaction more than raw 전문성. There is a gap between what HRD buyers screen on (career) and what drives learner outcomes (delivery and design). This is my synthesis, not a finding of any single paper.
- 성별 appearing as an attribute in the conjoint study implies demographic preferences exist in instructor choice, though its importance is lowest.

### Gaps
- Exact relative-importance % and part-worths of 권윤정·이영민 (2020); the KCI full text was unreachable.
- Results of 박지아 (2014) thesis.
- No AHP-weighted model of external instructor selection found for any sector (기업, 학교, 공공기관, 지자체).
- No survey-percentage data on how often criteria such as 평판, 추천, 강의평가 결과, or 커리큘럼 맞춤성 are used by 교육담당자.

## Q2. 강사 역량모델 (HRD 부서/기관이 쓰는 역량모델)

### Takeaway
Korean competency models for instructors mostly concern **사내강사** (in-house) and **평생교육 강사**. A typical model has 5–9 competency clusters spanning content expertise, instructional design, delivery/facilitation, and evaluation/reflection. These models are explicitly proposed as a basis for instructor **선발** (selection) and 양성.

### Cited Findings
- **국내 대기업 사내강사의 역량 및 교육 요구 분석** (이권재 외; KCI). Methods were an FGI with one firm's top instructors, an expert Delphi, and a survey of **235 사내강사**. Output: **9 역량, 36 행동지표**: 강사십(강사ship), 내용전문성, 정보수집 및 분석, 내용구조화, 교수설계 및 개발, 퍼실리테이션, 콘텐츠 개발, 평가 및 성찰, 과정마케팅. Education needs were highest for 평가 및 성찰, 교수설계 및 개발, and 정보수집 및 분석. Proposed as base data for 사내강사 선발·양성. — [KCI ART001896335](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART001896335); [교보 스콜라](https://scholar.kyobobook.co.kr/article/detail/4050025357670)
- **정상헌 (2018), 「사내강사의 역할 및 강의 역량에 관한 연구」, 학습자중심교과교육연구.** Four key variables affect 사내강사 effectiveness and 강의역량: **전문지식, 강의기술, 자세·태도, 핵심역량**. — [KCI ART002390943](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002390943)
- **박윤희 외 (2018), 「기업현장교사 역량모델 개발 연구」, HRD연구 20(2)** (일학습병행제 context). Roles are 교수설계자, 강사, 평가자, 상담자, 학습촉진자, 연구개발자. The 강사 role requires 강의 지도안 개발, 강의진행, 현장기술 지도, and 훈련활동 피드백. — [KCI ART002353452](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002353452); [KISS](https://kiss.kstudy.com/Detail/Ar?key=3608990)
- **박경희·한상훈 (2016), 「평생교육 강사의 핵심 교수역량모델 분석」, 예술인문사회융합멀티미디어논문지.** Survey of **125 평생교육 강사 in 대전**, content validity checked by 8 experts, then CFA and reliability testing. Output: **5 역량군, 20 하위역량**: 학습자 이해, 교육개발, 교수, 평가, 학습상담. — [KCI ART002092569](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002092569); [DBpia NODE11385347](https://www.dbpia.co.kr/journal/articleDetail?nodeId=NODE11385347)
- **평생교육 교수자의 교수 역량에 대한 교육 요구 분석** (Borich needs analysis). Top-priority needs were 학습 자원 및 정보 수집 역량, 교수매체 개발(·활용) 역량, and 프레젠테이션. — [KCI ART002261272](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002261272)
- **원격평생교육 교수자의 역할과 역량에 대한 요구분석.** Prioritizes the competencies remote lifelong-education instructors need; the specific ranks were not retrieved. — [KCI ART002813149](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002813149)
- **공무원 직무 전문성 향상을 위한 사내강사 교육과정체계 연구 (2024, 산업교육연구).** Proposes a public-sector in-house instructor system in stages: **선발 → 육성 → 활용 → 보상 → 확산**. — [KCI ART003126924](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART003126924)
- **정보보호 직무교육 사내강사 운영관리 방안 (디지털산업정보학회 논문지 14(4), 2018).** The management plan covers 강사 선발, 책임과 의무, 교육, 평가, and 지원. It recommends that internal rules specify the 선발 방침, 자격, 역할, and 해임 (dismissal), clarify 지원 자격 기준, and run a 자격 인증 제도 that grants status through 강의 평가. — [KoreaScience JAKO201810063224573 (PDF)](https://www.koreascience.or.kr/article/JAKO201810063224573.pdf)
- A practitioner example: HIRA's (건강보험심사평가원) education-system consulting report includes 사내강사 역량모델 진단 and 직급별 역량평가항목 개발. — [HIRA repository PDF](https://repository.hira.or.kr/bitstream/2019.oak/1627/3/HIRA%20%EA%B5%90%EC%9C%A1%EC%B2%B4%EA%B3%84%20%EC%88%98%EB%A6%BD%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EA%B5%90%EC%9C%A1%EA%B3%BC%EC%A0%95%20%EA%B0%9C%EB%B0%9C%20%EC%BB%A8%EC%84%A4%ED%8C%85.pdf)

### Inferences
- The competency models share a common core: **내용전문성 + 교수설계 + 전달/퍼실리테이션 + 평가·성찰 + 학습자 이해 + 태도(강사십)**. A buyer-side checklist for external instructors could map onto these clusters. No Korean paper was found that applies these models to vetting *external* instructors specifically.
- Instructors themselves report the largest development needs in 평가·성찰 and 교수설계. These are the areas least visible to a buyer who screens on 강의경력.

### Gaps
- No competency model built specifically for *외부강사/프리랜서 기업강사* as a buyer's selection tool was found.
- Weights or importance scores within the 9-competency (이권재 외) and 5-cluster (박경희·한상훈) models were not retrieved.

## Q3. 외부강사 관련 문제/실패 사례 (reported risks)

### Takeaway
The academic literature on failures is thin and scattered. Public-sector training studies flag **heavy dependence on 외래강사 as a quality-consistency risk**. School-sector studies (방과후학교) report problems in **채용·재직·재계약** processes and in teacher distrust of external instructors for subject teaching. Concrete incident-level risks (부적절 발언, 성편견 발언) are documented mainly in news reports, not peer-reviewed papers.

### Cited Findings
- **Public-sector training (공무원 교육):** an Incheon Office of Education research institute report on 지방교육행정공무원 전문성 notes that heavy reliance on 외래강사 makes 강사 확보 unstable and makes it hard to keep 교육 내용의 질 consistent. It proposes 전문강사요원 확충 and a 교육과정편성위원회. — [인천광역시교육청 교육정책연구소 (2024 upload) PDF](https://www.ice.go.kr/upload/ice/na/bbs_1360/2024/02/de94a3194b3531d96349b91807db0824.pdf)
- 사서직공무원 교육훈련 시스템 발전 방안 연구 (2011): the more 외래강사 a program uses, the harder it is to hold 교과 내용 and quality to a consistent level. — [KoreaScience JAKO201111436234650](https://www.koreascience.or.kr/article/JAKO201111436234650.pdf)
- **School / 방과후학교:**
  - 김달효 (2011), 「초등학교 '방과 후 학교' 외부강사의 채용과 재직 그리고 재계약 과정에서의 문제 분석」, 『교사교육연구』 50(2), pp.65–. The title confirms it analyzes problems across 채용, 재직, and 재계약; the abstract was not retrieved. It is cited in [KCI ART002020129](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002020129) and [KCI ART002254984](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002254984).
  - A Daejeon elementary-teacher perception study (ScienceON thesis DIKO0012273949): the share of 외부강사 in 방과후학교 is very high, but teachers do **not** want external instructors for 교과지도. — [ScienceON](https://scienceon.kisti.re.kr/srch/selectPORSrchArticle.do?cn=DIKO0012273949&dbt=DIKO)
  - 김혜경·권선희 (2012), a study of how external instructors perceive the elementary after-school English program (영어영문학 계열). — [KCI ART001724891](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART001724891)
  - 배현순·이희수 (2015), a survey of **236** Seoul elementary after-school instructors: higher 직무스트레스 goes with lower 직무만족, with 교사효능감 as mediator. This bears on the risk of instructor turnover and low quality. — [KCI ART002020129](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002020129)
  - KEDI forum proceedings (RM2008-14) include a section on the 운영실태 및 문제점 of 방과후학교 외부강사. — [KEDI](https://www.kedi.re.kr/khome/main/research/selectPubForm.do?plNum0=6337)
- **Career education (진로체험):** a study on 국가기록원/보존기록관 career-experience programs names 강사 역량 강화, 사전 교육 필요성, and 학교와의 협업 강화 as needs. It says instructors need both 직업적 전문성 ("정확하고 구체적인 지식") and 교육적 역량, and recommends external evaluation and a move to qualitative evaluation. — [한국기록관리학회지 25(3) PDF](https://accesson.kr/jksarm/assets/pdf/57115/journal-25-3-105.pdf). KRIVET research finds that the *quality* of career experience, proxied by 만족도, matters more for outcomes than mere participation. — [KRIVET 2022 패널 학술대회 PDF](https://www.krivet.re.kr/repository/bitstream/202405/5931/2/KC1711_fulltext.pdf)
- **Non-academic but relevant incident evidence (news, for context only):**
  - Ulsan 2018: a sex-education instructor from a private organization used gender-biased remarks, and similar language was found in **8 of 10 classes** in the school's own review. The city took a written explanation and excluded the organization from the next year's 공모사업. — [뉴시스 2018-11-23](https://www.newsis.com/view/?id=NISX20181123_0000482298)
  - 2026 national survey of freelance sex-education instructors (instructor side): 59% experienced disruption by learners and 54.4% experienced undue interference from the commissioning institution. — [경향신문 2026-03-01](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202603012043015/)
  - Education commentators call for systematizing 외부강사 자격요건 and continuous 평가·관리 as school programs expand. — [충청투데이](https://www.cctoday.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=2203210)
  - Outsourced 방과후 operation: when vendor selection is price-first, the vendor takes excessive commission, good instructors leave, instructors change often, and class quality drops. — [Daum/매일경제 2025-05-10](https://v.daum.net/v/20250510091500416)

### Inferences
- The failure modes visible in the Korean literature are mostly **structural**: dependence on outside instructors leads to inconsistent quality; short contracts lead to turnover; price-first outsourcing loses good instructors; network-based hiring leaves no systematic screen. **Individual-conduct** failures (부적절 발언, 성편견) appear in news rather than peer-reviewed studies.
- I found no Korean academic study quantifying failures such as 노쇼, 내용 불일치, or 수준 미스매치 from the buyer's (교육담당자) side.

### Gaps
- No survey of HRD/교육담당자 on the frequency of specific external-instructor failures (no-show, off-brief content, level mismatch, inappropriate remarks).
- The abstract and findings of 김달효 (2011) were not retrieved.

## Q4. 강사 품질 검증 방식 (강사 풀/인력뱅크, 추천, 시강, 강의평가)

### Takeaway
In practice, verification runs on (a) **personal networks and referrals** in corporate HRD, identified as the core problem by 권윤정·이영민 (2020); (b) **municipal 강사은행/강사뱅크 registries** with low entry thresholds in lifelong education; (c) **formal criteria-based selection plus criminal-record checks** in schools (방과후학교); and (d) **강의평가-based certification** proposed for in-house instructors. No Korean academic evaluation of how well 강사은행 work was found.

### Cited Findings
- Corporate: selection is "비체계적이고 개인 네트워크에 의존". — [KCI ART002629706](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002629706). The freelance-instructor thesis models 사회적 네트워크 (and online networks) as a determinant of being hired. — [Hansung dspace](https://dspace.hansung.ac.kr/bitstream/2024.oak/6717/2/200000726727.pdf)
- 지자체 평생학습 강사은행 (administrative pages, not academic):
  - 천안시: registration requires a relevant 자격증 *or* ≥1 year of teaching experience in the field, and one mandatory 강사연수 after registration before being assigned to 찾아가는 평생학습 courses. — [천안시 평생학습포털](https://www.cheonan.go.kr/lll/sub05_01.do)
  - 구리시: anyone with relevant 전문성 may register, and the bank also provides 전문성 함양 programs. — [구리시 평생학습관](https://www.guri.go.kr/lll/contents.do?key=1534)
  - 예산군 flow: 강사등록 → 강사정보 DB구축 → 강사은행 → 강의의뢰. — [예산군](https://yesan.go.kr/edu/sub02_02_01.do). 원주시: 등록신청 → 심사 및 등록 → 기관연결. — [원주시](https://www.wonju.go.kr/culture/contents.do?key=5022). 예천군 deletes a registration if documents are not submitted within 14 days. — [예천군](https://www.ycg.kr/open.content/lifelong/instructor/bank.system/)
  - 광주시 describes the 강사은행제 as systematizing instructor information to link it to institutions. — [광주시](https://www.gjcity.go.kr/jumin/jumin/contents.do?mId=0402000000)
- Schools: the 2022 방과후학교 길라잡이 (부산 남부교육지원청) requires a 강사 선정 계획 that sets out the 자격 기준, 선정 방법, 심사 기준, and 재계약 기준, plus 성범죄 경력 and 아동학대 관련 범죄 전력 조회 for external instructors. — [방과후학교 길라잡이 2022 PDF](https://home.pen.go.kr/upload/nambu/na/bbs_3894/ntt_670858/doc_5783v6f8c-59v02-4fvce-96vad-045evd7aeva601_v7241.pdf). Instructors report re-contracting every year, semester, or even quarter, with document submission and an interview each time. — [매일노동뉴스](https://www.labortoday.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=161543)
- Career experience: the 교육부 runs a 진로체험 인증제 that screens and certifies free career-experience providers, to build quality control of 체험처. — [KRIVET 직업체험 중심 진로교육 지원체제 연구 PDF](https://www.krivet.re.kr/repository/bitstream/202405/89/2/A21119_fulltext.pdf)
- In-house: the 2018 정보보호 사내강사 paper proposes 자격 인증 through 강의 평가 and explicit 해임 rules. — [KoreaScience PDF](https://www.koreascience.or.kr/article/JAKO201810063224573.pdf)
- Practitioner (non-academic) vetting checklist: prior lectures in the same field, actual lecture video or demo (시연), sample 교안, and evaluations from past clients. 강사료 depends on lecture hours, career, whether 교안 must be developed, and travel distance. — [나눔콘 용어사전](https://nanumcon.com/term/instructor-sourcing)

### Inferences
- The 강사은행 model sets low entry bars (자격증 or 1 year of experience) and works as a directory, not a quality screen. Quality verification is left to the commissioning institution.
- 시강 (demo lectures) and structured use of past 강의평가 data appear in practitioner guidance but not in the Korean academic literature found.

### Gaps
- No academic evaluation of 강사은행/인력뱅크 effectiveness (utilization, quality outcomes).
- No Korean data on how often HRD staff use 시강, reference checks, or past 강의평가 scores when hiring external instructors.
- Sector coverage is uneven: corporate (1 direct study), lifelong education (competency and evaluation studies), schools (process problems), and public agencies (brief mentions in policy reports). University 초빙강사 selection was not covered.
